BARNOOTAAI360

BARNOOTAAI360 is a multilingual, offline-first learning ecosystem concept for Ethiopian Grades 9–12, designed to support students with digital learning resources and AI-assisted study.

In developmentGrades 9–12Multilingual UXAndroidPostgreSQLOffline-first direction

Learning problem and product direction

Students need accessible learning resources that work across languages and connectivity conditions. BARNOOTAAI360 explores a learning experience for Ethiopian Grades 9–12 with digital resources, structured learning activities and AI-assisted study support.

Its design direction includes multilingual student experiences and an offline-capable Android client. Offline-first is a product goal: actual offline coverage depends on which content is stored locally and how synchronization is implemented. AI services may still need a network unless on-device inference is built.

Engineering approach

  • Web and Android student experiences connected through application APIs.
  • Application services organized around authentication, learning, quizzes and resources.
  • Role-based access control to separate user permissions.
  • PostgreSQL for structured application data and JSON content storage where appropriate.
  • Room database direction for Android local persistence and later synchronization.
