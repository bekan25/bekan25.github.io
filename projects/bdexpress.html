Product direction

The public repository describes a marketplace concept with buyer and seller journeys, product listings, search and filtering, cart and wishlist, order tracking, and seller tools. These are repository-described goals and features; this page does not imply that every integration is complete or deployed.

Engineering considerations

  • Consistent language switching across the customer journey.
  • Search and filtering that remain usable on mobile screens.
  • Clear order states and transparent delivery status.
  • Payment and notification providers must be configured and tested before being described as live.
